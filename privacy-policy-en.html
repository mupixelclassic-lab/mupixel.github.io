PIXELORE · MU PIXEL

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. Data Controller

Mu Pixel is developed and published under the Pixelore brand. The data controller is Sam, the creator and publisher of Mu Pixel.

For privacy and personal data inquiries, contact: mupixelclassic@gmail.com.

2. Information We Process

When using Mu Pixel, we may process:

  • account information: username, email address and hashed password;
  • game data: characters, game progress, items and settings;
  • private messages sent through the game.

We do not maintain our own permanent logs containing users' IP addresses. This information is used to manage accounts, operate the game, save game progress, enable communication between players, maintain security and resolve technical issues.

3. Data Storage

Game data is stored in a MySQL database on a server located in Poland, using hosting infrastructure provided by KORBANK S.A.

Passwords are not stored in plain text. They are protected using a salted password-hashing function.

Private messages are stored for 30 days. Other chat messages are processed in server memory and are not permanently stored in the database.

We create backups as follows: hourly backups are retained for 24 hours, and daily backups are retained for 7 days.

Account and game data are retained until the account is deleted or the purpose for processing the data no longer applies, subject to applicable legal obligations. Data in backups is subject to the retention schedule described above.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure and service providers necessary to host and operate the game, including the hosting provider. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or when necessary to protect the security of the game and its users.

5. Messages and Security

Game administrators may access private messages in justified circumstances, particularly when investigating reports, violations of game rules or security issues.

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss or disclosure.

6. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data by emailing mupixelclassic@gmail.com. Instructions are available on the Account Deletion page.

After verifying a request, we will delete the account and associated data, except for information that must be retained by law. Data in backups will be removed according to the backup rotation schedule.

7. Age Requirements

Mu Pixel is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users' personal information is processed in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws, including rules concerning minors.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, particularly when game features, data processing practices or applicable laws change. The latest version will be published at the same website address.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the processing of personal information, please contact mupixelclassic@gmail.com.

Pre-publication note: The pseudonym “Sam” is used as supplied by the publisher. Before publication, verify whether this identification satisfies the data controller transparency requirements applicable under GDPR.
